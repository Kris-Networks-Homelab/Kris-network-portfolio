# About Me

I’m Kris, a technical professional transitioning into network engineering after a long career in the trades.

Trade work taught me how to diagnose faults under real-world pressure, interpret technical information, follow safe and methodical processes, document work clearly, and take responsibility for the result. Those habits transfer directly to network troubleshooting: establish the symptoms, isolate the fault domain, test assumptions, make controlled changes, and verify service restoration.

I am completing a Bachelor of Information Technology focused on Network Engineering and preparing for the Cisco CCNA. Alongside that study, I maintain a physical Cisco homelab with routers, a managed switch, and Linux endpoints. I use it to practise Cisco IOS, routing, switching, addressing, infrastructure documentation, and troubleshooting on real equipment.

Cisco Packet Tracer complements the physical lab by letting me design and test broader scenarios involving VLANs, trunks, inter-VLAN routing, OSPF, NAT/PAT, DHCP, ACLs, and wireless infrastructure concepts.

I am seeking an entry point in network operations or infrastructure where I can contribute that practical mindset and continue developing hands-on capability.
