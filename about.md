# About Me

Hi, I'm Kris, an ICT student focused on building a career as a Network Engineer.

I enjoy building, documenting, and troubleshooting real systems. To develop practical networking skills, I maintain a Cisco-based homelab where I experiment with routing, switching, and network design concepts while studying toward the CCNA certification.

Through my university studies and independent lab work, I’ve been developing hands-on experience with network configuration, infrastructure documentation, and troubleshooting methodologies. I also enjoy working with Linux environments to support networking and system administration tasks.

I’m particularly interested in routing and switching, network architecture, and diagnosing complex connectivity issues in real-world environments.

My goal is to transition into an entry-level networking or infrastructure role, where I can continue developing practical engineering skills while contributing to reliable and well-designed network systems.
